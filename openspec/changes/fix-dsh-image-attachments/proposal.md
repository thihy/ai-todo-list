## Why
Image-only messages cannot be sent, and composer attachments never show thumbnails.

## What Changes
- Allow image-only submissions, including pending clipboard imports.
- Display indexed raster previews in the composer and historical turns.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `ai-image-attachments`: image-only submission and durable attachment previews.
### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
AIComposer, AIPane, composer inbox and the existing attachment protocol. No new dependencies.
